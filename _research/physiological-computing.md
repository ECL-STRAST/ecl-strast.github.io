Questionnaires tell us how people say they feel; physiological signals
tell us how their body responds. We use heart rate variability (HRV) as an
objective marker of stress, both to evaluate training technology and to
look after the people who work in healthcare.

## Stress during simulated procedures

HRV recorded during VR and 3D-printed pericardiocentesis training shows
whether a simulator reproduces the stress of the real procedure, and how
demographic, lifestyle and academic factors shape each trainee's
response.

## Predicting burnout

Burnout is a serious problem among nurses and nursing assistants,
particularly in intensive care. Combining HRV with validated burnout
scales and psychosocial and occupational factors, we build predictive
models for the early detection of burnout risk.
