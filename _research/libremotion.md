Quantitative movement analysis is central to clinical evaluation and
rehabilitation, but it has traditionally required dedicated labs with
optical systems and force platforms. LibreMotion is our effort to
democratise the capture, storage and analysis of biomechanical data, with
a focus on data from low-cost devices such as VR headsets.

## LibreMotion Capture

A motion capture library for virtual reality devices that records the
kinematics of the user from the headset and controllers, so any VR
application can become a measurement tool.
[Source code](https://github.com/ECL-STRAST/LibreMotion-Capture).

## CHLOE

CHLOE (Clinical Helper for Locomotion Objective Evaluation) is a
browser-based viewer for biomechanical data in C3D format, the standard of
biomechanics labs, including analog signals such as EMG. It needs almost no
configuration and focuses on the tasks physiotherapists perform most often.
[Source code](https://github.com/ECL-STRAST/LibreMotion-CHLOE).
