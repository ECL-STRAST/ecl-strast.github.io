Some invasive procedures are rare in clinical practice but must be done
right the first time. Pericardiocentesis (draining fluid from the sac
around the heart, often to relieve cardiac tamponade) needs anatomical
knowledge, ultrasound guidance and precise needle placement. Simulation
lets students practise it safely and repeatedly.

## VR and 3D-printed pericardiocentesis

Together with clinicians, we designed two custom simulators: an immersive
VR simulation and a 3D-printed mannequin. With cohorts of final-year
medical students we have compared them on learning outcomes (OSCE),
cognitive load, usability, presence and simulator sickness, and on
physiological stress measured through heart rate variability. Both
simulators reproduce stress levels comparable to clinical scenarios, and the
VR simulation was rated highly usable.
