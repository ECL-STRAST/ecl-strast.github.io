Patients with upper-limb disorders need long, repetitive rehabilitation,
which is a heavy burden for them and for health systems. Virtual reality
can move part of that therapy home: headsets are affordable, immersive,
track motion accurately, and can run custom applications that a therapist
supervises remotely.

## FarmDay

FarmDay is a bimanual training application built around activities of
daily living on a farm. It runs on commodity hardware with a real-time
remote setup, so a therapist can follow the session from elsewhere. Its six
activities were evaluated by physiotherapists specialised in neuromotor
disorders, functional rehabilitation and occupational therapy, and scored
86 on the System Usability Scale.
