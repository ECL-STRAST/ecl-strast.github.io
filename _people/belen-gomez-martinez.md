Belén developed CHLOE (Clinical Helper for Locomotion Objective
Evaluation), a web application for the visualisation of biomechanical
data of physiotherapy patients, as her bachelor thesis in Biomedical
Engineering. It is part of the [LibreMotion](/research/libremotion/)
project.
