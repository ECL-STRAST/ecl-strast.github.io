Rodrigo leads the Embodied Computing Lab. His research brings together
software engineering, virtual reality and biomedical engineering to build
technology that measures, trains and supports people in healthcare
settings: VR instruments for musculoskeletal assessment, gamified
rehabilitation, simulators for medical training, low-cost open motion
capture, and remote rendering for VR.

He supervises bachelor, master and doctoral theses, many of them in
Biomedical Engineering, and works closely with physiotherapists and
clinicians so that the group's tools are validated where they will be
used.
