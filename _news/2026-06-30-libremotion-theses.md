Congratulations to Miguel Ángel Durán Luque and Belén Gómez Martínez, whose
bachelor theses in Biomedical Engineering were both graded 10/10. Miguel
Ángel built LibreMotion Capture, a motion capture library for VR devices,
and Belén built CHLOE, a web viewer for biomechanical data designed for
physiotherapists. Both are part of our [LibreMotion](/research/libremotion/)
effort to democratise biomechanical analysis.
