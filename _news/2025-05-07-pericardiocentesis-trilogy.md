Our work on pericardiocentesis simulation has produced three papers this
spring, in Scientific Reports, Advances in Simulation and JMIR Serious
Games. Together they compare our VR and 3D-printed simulators on stress,
learning outcomes and usability with more than a hundred final-year
medical students.
