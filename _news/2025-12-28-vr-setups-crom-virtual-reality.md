Our comparison of three VR setups (HTC Vive Pro Eye, HP Reverb G2 and Meta
Quest 2) for cervical range of motion assessment is published in Virtual
Reality. Across 94 sessions all three were reliable, and the low-cost
devices did not fall behind the high-end one.
