Our pilot study on the feasibility of the Meta Quest 2 as an upper-limb
motion capture system is out in Scientific Reports. Compared against a
Vicon optical system, the controllers' wrist trajectories match closely
while the hands stay in the headset's field of view, and degrade when they
leave it: a key constraint for anyone using inside-out VR tracking in
rehabilitation.
